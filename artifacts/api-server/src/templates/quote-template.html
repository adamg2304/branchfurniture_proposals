White Glove Delivery, in plain terms

Your quote includes our full-service delivery and installation. Here's exactly where the line is drawn, with no surprises on install day.

Included in your quote

  • Scheduled delivery window coordinated with your building
  • Certificate of Insurance (COI) handled by Branch
  • Professional install team: full assembly of every item
  • In-room placement according to your space plan
  • Complete packaging removal and clean-up
  • Single point of contact: your Branch Captain coordinates it all

Not included (and when extra fees apply)

  • Electrical or plumbing connections, never performed by Branch or its partners
  • Union labor, where required by your building (surcharge applies)
  • After-hours or weekend delivery (surcharge applies)
  • Carrying furniture up or through stairwells (surcharge applies)
  • Site readiness: premises must be clear, safe, and construction-complete
About Branch

Hi, we're Branch,
and this is our story.

After furnishing dozens of offices for a commercial real estate startup, our CEO Greg Hayes realized the average office furniture company falls into one of two categories: high quality, but expensive and slow, or affordable and fast but lacking in quality and service.

There wasn't a great option for individuals and teams who wanted to furnish an inspiring office without taking forever or breaking the bank.

That's why we founded Branch: to make it easy for people and teams to create inspiring offices. We sell direct, so our collection costs half as much as premium furniture of comparable quality, and we design our products with your well-being and work day in mind.

We provide end-to-end services: design, delivery, white glove installation, and even the ability to trade in used furniture when you move or grow. We believe it's a better approach to the way we work today.

Terms & Conditions The fine print, in plain sight. ›
1. Quote validity

Quotes/Invoices are valid for 30 days, after which product pricing is subject to change.

2. Floor plans

Should there be a predetermined furniture layout ("Floor Plan") for your office space, all furniture will be assembled and placed according to the Floor Plan. Branch is not liable for delivery problems arising from inaccuracies in the Floor Plan. When a discrepancy arises between the Floor Plan and Quote, the Quote is the source of truth.

3. Payments

Orders under $15,000 require payment in full prior to processing. Orders over $15,000 require a minimum 50% deposit prior to processing, with the remaining balance due at least 5 business days before delivery. Payment is deemed received once funds reflect in Branch's bank account. Credit card payments are subject to a 2.9% processing fee. Purchase Order payment terms supersede standard Branch terms where agreed in writing prior to processing.

4. Insurance

Clients will ensure they hold adequate insurance to facilitate furniture delivery and installation in the premises, as required by building management.

5. Delivery options

White Glove Delivery: delivery and installation of products in your premises. A standard 15% installation fee applies. Additional fees may apply where union labor, after-hours delivery, or movement of furniture through stairwells is required. Branch and its partners do not connect electrical or plumbing services.

Standard Delivery: receipt via parcel or LTL shipment. Clients are responsible for assembly and packaging disposal. Freight/LTL shipments are unloaded to a ground-level area outside your office; clients transport items into the premises.

6. White glove installation

Enterprise quotes include White Glove installation fees except where Standard Delivery is requested. An additional installation fee may be charged for changes made to an order after it has been placed.

7. Condition of job site

Client is responsible for ensuring the premises can receive furniture. Branch may refuse delivery to premises undergoing construction, with areas that aren't clear, or deemed unsafe. Additional charges for redelivery and storage are the client's responsibility.

8. Delivery information

Client will provide the correct delivery address, building delivery hours, union labor requirements, elevator booking requirements, and Certificate of Insurance requirements. Inaccurate information may result in delays, service changes, or additional fees.

9. Damages upon delivery

Client is responsible for inspecting delivery and noting missing, incorrect, or damaged items. Report damages to the installation team or enterprise@branchfurniture.com within 24 hours of delivery. Branch will respond with remedy action in a reasonable and timely manner.

10. Returns

Returns accepted within 30 days of purchase. Custom products, shipping fees, and installation fees are non-refundable. Products must be in like-new condition. Return shipping costs and a 20% restocking fee are deducted from the refund. Products shipped outside the contiguous US or Canadian provinces are final sale.
